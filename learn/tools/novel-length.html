How Long Should My Novel Be?

Discover target word counts for sixteen categories of fiction, and see where your manuscript lands within those ranges.

The short answer

Most adult novels run 70,000 to 100,000 words, and 90,000 is a safe target when you have nothing else to go on. Genre changes that figure a good deal. A cozy mystery is comfortable at 70,000, while epic fantasy is often expected to run past 120,000.

Below 50,000 words, a book reads as a novella to most trade publishers. Above 120,000 it becomes a harder sell for a debut, because a longer book costs more to edit, print, and ship.

In range 95,000 words sits inside the usual range for adult fantasy.

Usual range 90,000 to 120,000. Your target: 100,000

Ranges reflect what agents and editors commonly expect for a book being sold into the trade market. These ranges are conventions rather than rules, and they may shift over time or between publishers.

Short story, novelette, novella, novel

If you are working below novel length, the boundaries become quite precise (mostly because awards need to draw the lines). These are the categories that the Science Fiction and Fantasy Writers Association uses for the Nebula Awards, and the rest of publishing has largely adopted them.

A book can clear the 40,000 word bar and still be too short to sell as an adult novel. The award category and the market expectation are different things.

Why the ranges exist

None of these numbers are actually about art, but instead are mostly about cost and shelf space. A longer book takes more editing, more paper, and more shipping weight, but it takes up the same slot in a catalogue as a shorter one that costs less to make. A publisher betting on an unknown writer would rather that bet be cheap.

Readers exert their own pressure. Someone picking up a cozy mystery has an expectation that they will be able to finish the novel in a few days to a week. Someone picking up epic fantasy is likely buying a few weeks to a month, and would feel shortchanged by 80,000 words. The ranges are roughly where those two pressures have settled.

Debuts get less room. An established author can turn in 180,000 words and be trusted. A first novel at the same length reads as a manuscript nobody has edited yet. If you are querying, aim for the middle of your range rather than the top of it.

When your draft lands outside the range

If your finished draft falls outside the typical range, it could mean a few things.

Too short usually means the story is thinner than it looks. For example, there may be a subplot that never developed, a supporting cast doing very little, or a conflict that resolves too easily. Padding a 55,000 word draft up to 85,000 with description and exposition will not fix it. Find the missing threads to build a richer, deeper narrative.

Too long is more often a structural fix. A draft 40,000 words over its range usually has a whole narrative thread that could be removed, two characters who could be one, or a first act that begins a chapter (or three) before the story actually gets going. Line editing will recover a few thousand words, but cutting a superfluous subplot could recover tens of thousands.

You could start by counting the scenes before you count the words. The reverse outline is the fastest way to see which parts of a draft are carrying their weight.

Questions writers ask

How long should a novel be?
Most adult novels run 70,000 to 100,000 words, and 90,000 is a safe target if you have no other information. Below 50,000 a book reads as a novella to most trade publishers, and above 120,000 it becomes a hard sell for a debut because it costs more to print and edit.
How many words should a fantasy novel be?
Adult fantasy usually runs 90,000 to 120,000 words, with 100,000 a reliable target. Epic fantasy runs longer, 120,000 to 160,000, though a debut is held closer to 120,000 than to 160,000 no matter how large the world is.
Is 50,000 words enough for a novel?
It clears the technical bar. The Science Fiction and Fantasy Writers Association counts anything from 40,000 words as a novel. It is short of what most adult trade publishers expect, though it is a normal length for category romance, and comfortably long for middle grade. A 50,000 word NaNoWriMo draft is usually a complete story that needs another pass rather than a finished novel.
How long should a first novel be?
Closer to the middle of your genre's range than to either edge. Debut authors get less latitude on length than established ones, because an unproven book at 150,000 words is a larger bet for a publisher than the same book at 100,000.
What should I do if my novel is too long?
Look for a structural cause before you start trimming sentences. A draft 40,000 words over its range usually has a subplot that could be cut whole, a cast that could be combined, or a first act that starts too early. Line editing recovers a few thousand words. Cutting a thread recovers tens of thousands.
Does word count matter if I am self-publishing?
There is no gatekeeper to satisfy, so the hard limits disappear. Reader expectations do not. Someone buying a cozy mystery expects an afternoon of reading, and someone buying epic fantasy expects a week of it, so the ranges are still worth knowing even when nobody is enforcing them.
