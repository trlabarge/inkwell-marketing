Convert Your Word Count to Pages

Calculate how many pages your novel runs to, as a manuscript and as a printed book.

Free, and nothing to sign up for

The short answer

In standard manuscript format (12 point Times New Roman or Courier, double-spaced, one inch margins), a page holds about 250 words. So 80,000 words is roughly 320 manuscript pages.

A printed book sets more words on a page, usually 250 to 350, so the same novel arrives at somewhere near 228 to 320 printed pages. Agents and editors typically work in manuscript pages, but also will want your word count.

Where 250 words a page comes from

Standard manuscript format is a set of conventions publishing settled on long before anyone submitted by email: 12 point Courier or Times New Roman, double-spaced, one inch margins on all four sides, first line of each paragraph indented half an inch.

Set a page that way and it holds roughly 250 words. Courier is a monospaced font, so it lands almost exactly there. Times New Roman is narrower and often runs closer to 300. The industry kept 250 anyway, because the point was never precision. The point was that an editor could pick up any manuscript, glance at the stack, and know roughly what they were holding.

Why anyone still cares. Nobody prints submissions any more, so the page count is mostly a habit. What survived is the estimate: when a writer says "a 320 page novel", everyone in publishing hears 80,000 words. The conversion is a shared shorthand, not a formatting requirement.

Manuscript pages and book pages are different numbers

A double-spaced manuscript page holds about 250 words. A typeset page in a finished book holds 250 to 350, because it is single-spaced, set in a smaller measure, and printed on a smaller sheet than US Letter.

So the same 80,000 word novel is a 320 page manuscript and roughly a 230 to 320 page book. Neither number is wrong. They are measuring different objects.

The printed range is wide for good reasons. A mass market paperback with small type and tight leading fits far more on a page than a large print hardcover. Trim size, font, leading, and how generous the designer is with chapter openings all move the number. If you need an exact page count for a finished book, only your typesetter can give you one.

Which number to give an agent

Give them a word count. Almost every agent guideline and submission form asks for words, not pages, and for a practical reason: a word count is the same number on every machine that opens the file, and a page count is not.

Round to the nearest thousand when you quote it. A query letter that says 80,000 words reads as a professional who knows the conventions. One that says 79,847 words reads as someone who has not finished revising, because the specificity is doing no work.

If a guideline does ask for pages, they mean manuscript pages in standard format. Use the 250 word figure and give a round number.

Questions writers ask

How many pages is 80,000 words?
About 320 pages in standard manuscript format, which is double-spaced at roughly 250 words per page. As a printed book the same novel lands around 228 to 320 pages, because a typeset page holds more words than a double-spaced manuscript page.
How many words is 300 pages?
About 75,000 words as a double-spaced manuscript, at 250 words per page. If you mean 300 pages of a printed book, it is closer to 75,000 to 105,000 words, depending on the trim size and how the book is typeset.
Do agents and editors want manuscript pages or printed book pages?
Manuscript pages, though most of them ask for a word count rather than a page count. Word count is the number that does not change when someone opens your file with different fonts or margins, which is why the industry runs on it.
Is 250 words per page still accurate in Times New Roman?
It is a convention rather than a literal count. Courier at 12 point lands very close to 250 words per page. Times New Roman is narrower and often runs nearer 250 to 300. The convention exists so that everyone estimates the same way, so use 250 and do not worry about the drift.
How long does it take to read 80,000 words?
Roughly 5 hours and 20 minutes at 250 words per minute, which is a typical adult reading speed for fiction. Readers vary widely, so treat it as an estimate rather than a promise.
Should I track my progress in words or pages?
Words. A page count moves when you change the font, the margins, or the line spacing, so it measures your formatting as much as your writing. A word count only moves when you write.
