How Many Pages Is Your Word Count?

Convert any word count into single-spaced, double-spaced, and printed book pages, on US Letter or A4.

Calculator6 min read

The short answer

A double-spaced page in 12 point Times New Roman with one inch margins holds about 250 words, and a single-spaced page about 500. So 300 words is about 1.2 double-spaced pages, 1,000 words is 4, and an 80,000 word novel is about 320. An A4 page holds about 4% more.

A printed book page holds more, about 275 to 375 words averaged across the book, so that same novel becomes roughly 213 to 291 book pages.

How many words fit on a single-spaced or double-spaced page?

About 500 words single-spaced and 250 words double-spaced, in 12 point Times New Roman with one inch margins on US Letter. Double spacing halves the number of lines on the page and leaves everything else alone, which is why the second figure is exactly half the first.

The arithmetic, if you want to check it. A US Letter page with one inch margins leaves a text block 6.5 inches wide and 9 inches tall. In 12 point Times New Roman that fits around 46 single-spaced lines, or about 23 double-spaced ones, at 11 to 12 words a line. Twenty-three lines of 11 words is 253 words, which is where the 250 figure lives. Twelve words a line gives 276, which is why some calculators say 275 or 300.

Schools and universities that set an assignment by pages almost always mean double-spaced, 12 point, one inch margins. A two page essay is about 500 words, a five page paper about 1,250, and a ten page paper about 2,500. If the brief gives a word count as well, the word count wins.

Does A4 paper change the page count?

A little. A4 (210 by 297 mm) is about 0.24 inches narrower than US Letter and 0.69 inches taller. With the same one inch (2.54 cm) margins, its text block is 6.27 by 9.69 inches instead of 6.5 by 9.

The lines are 3.5% shorter (6.27 ÷ 6.5 = 0.965) and there are 7.7% more of them (9.69 ÷ 9 = 1.077). Multiply the two and an A4 page holds about 4% more words (0.965 × 1.077 = 1.039). That makes it roughly 260 words double-spaced and 520 single-spaced.

For anything under a few thousand words the difference rounds away. For a novel it shows: 80,000 words is about 320 pages on Letter and 308 on A4. Use the toggle in the calculator to see your own count on either size.

Which size you are using. Word processors pick the default from your region. If you are in the United States or Canada you are almost certainly on Letter. Most of the rest of the world, including the UK, Europe, India, Brazil, and Indonesia, defaults to A4. Check under page setup if you are unsure.

What else changes how many words fit on a page?

The 250 and 500 figures are conventions, not measurements of your document. These are the things that move your real count, roughly in order of how much they matter:

  • Line spacing. Double spacing halves the words on a page. The 1.15 spacing many word processors use by default sits in between.
  • Font and size. Times New Roman is narrow, so it fits more than wider faces such as Arial or Verdana at the same size. Courier, a monospaced font, lands almost exactly on 250 double-spaced. Moving from 12 point to 11 adds noticeably more words to every line.
  • Margins. Wider margins shorten every line and remove a few lines from the page.
  • Paragraphs and dialogue. Fiction with a lot of dialogue has many short lines, and each one leaves empty space at its end. A dialogue-heavy chapter can run to noticeably more pages than a dense one of the same length.
  • Headings, chapter breaks, and scene breaks. Most manuscripts start each chapter on a new page, a third of the way down. A novel with sixty short chapters loses a lot of page to white space.

If you need an exact figure for your own document, your word processor already has it: set your font, spacing, and margins, and read the page count at the bottom of the window. Everything on this page is the estimate you use before that, or when the formatting is not yours to choose.

Where 250 words a page comes from

Standard manuscript format is a set of conventions publishing settled on long before anyone submitted by email: 12 point Courier or Times New Roman, double-spaced, one inch margins on all four sides, first line of each paragraph indented half an inch.

Set a page that way and it holds roughly 250 words. Courier lands almost exactly there. Times New Roman often runs closer to 275 or 300. The industry kept 250 anyway, because the point was never precision. The point was that an editor could pick up any manuscript, glance at the stack, and know roughly what they were holding. When a writer says "a 320 page novel", everyone in publishing still hears 80,000 words.

Why manuscript pages and book pages differ

A double-spaced manuscript page holds about 250 words. A typeset page in a finished book holds more, because it is single-spaced and set in smaller type, even though the page itself is smaller than Letter or A4. So the same 80,000 word novel is a 320 page manuscript and roughly a 213 to 291 page book. Neither number is wrong. They are measuring different objects.

Where the book figure comes from: a common trade paperback trim of 5.5 by 8.5 inches leaves a text block of about 4.25 by 7 inches. Set in 11 point type on 14 point leading, that is 36 lines (7 inches is 504 points, divided by 14), at 9 to 11 words a line, so a full page of prose holds about 325 to 400 words. Dialogue, chapter openings, and scene breaks leave white space, which pulls the average across a whole book down to roughly 275 to 375 words a page. That is the range the calculator uses.

A mass market paperback, a large print edition, or a generous hardcover design can all land outside it. Trim size, font, leading, and how much room the designer gives chapter openings all move the number. If you need an exact page count for a finished book, only your typesetter can give you one.

Which number to give an agent

Give them a word count. Almost every agent guideline and submission form asks for words, not pages, and for a practical reason: a word count is the same number on every machine that opens the file, and a page count is not.

Round to the nearest thousand when you quote it. A query letter that says 80,000 words reads as a professional who knows the conventions. One that says 79,847 words reads as someone who has not finished revising, because the specificity is doing no work.

If a guideline does ask for pages, they mean manuscript pages in standard format. Use the 250 word figure and give a round number.

Questions writers ask

How many pages is 300 words?
About 1.2 double-spaced pages, or just over half a single-spaced page (0.6), in 12 point Times New Roman with one inch margins. A4 paper gives the same answer at this length: 1.2 double-spaced, 0.6 single-spaced.
How many pages is 1,000 words?
About 4 double-spaced pages or 2 single-spaced pages on US Letter. On A4, which holds slightly more per page, it is about 3.8 double-spaced and 1.9 single-spaced.
How many pages is 80,000 words?
About 320 pages in standard manuscript format, which is double-spaced at roughly 250 words per page. As a printed book the same novel lands around 213 to 291 pages, because a typeset page holds more words than a double-spaced manuscript page.
Does A4 paper change the page count?
A little. With the same one inch (2.54 cm) margins, an A4 page holds about 4% more words than US Letter: roughly 260 double-spaced and 520 single-spaced. The difference only shows in long documents. 80,000 words is about 320 Letter pages and about 308 A4 pages.
How many lines is 250 words?
About 21 to 23 lines, at 11 to 12 words a line in 12 point Times New Roman with one inch margins. Double-spaced, that is roughly one full page. Single-spaced, it is about half a page.
Do agents and editors want manuscript pages or printed book pages?
Manuscript pages, though most of them ask for a word count rather than a page count. Word count is the number that does not change when someone opens your file with different fonts or margins, which is why the industry runs on it.
